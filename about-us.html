About Radhey Radhey RO Service

Radhey Radhey RO Service is a trusted, independent multi-brand water purifier service and maintenance provider operating across multiple cities in India. We are dedicated to ensuring that every household and commercial facility has continuous access to clean, healthy, and high-purity drinking water.

Who We Are

We are a network of trained and skilled independent technicians who specialize in the repair, filter replacement, installation, and preventative maintenance of domestic and commercial multi-stage reverse osmosis (RO) systems.

Our Core Commitments

  • Experienced Independent Technicians: Skilled professionals equipped with digital TDS meters and diagnostic tools to ensure accurate fault resolution.
  • High-Grade Compatible Components: We use reliable, factory-tested replacement membranes and filtration cartridges to maximize system longevity and water safety.
  • Transparent Upfront Pricing: Fixed inspection rates with no hidden costs or surprise charges. Upfront diagnosis is provided before starting any work.
  • Multi-City Doorstep Network: Serving households across major metropolitan and urban centres with fast technician dispatch.

Independent Operation Disclosure

Radhey Radhey RO Service operates as an independent third-party repair and maintenance service. We provide multi-brand compatibility services and are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or an authorized service centre for any single brand or manufacturer.
